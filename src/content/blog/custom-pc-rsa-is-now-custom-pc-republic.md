**Custom PC RSA has a new name: Custom PC Republic.** Our name has changed because our work has grown.

## Where we started

We began in **2012**, building and selling **custom computers** in South Africa. Customers kept coming back for more than hardware: help with Windows, email, networks and "can you just look at this?"

## Where we are now

Today we help **individuals and small-to-medium enterprises around the world** with:

- 🛠️ **Help desk & tech support**, from break & fix to fully managed
- ☁️ **Microsoft 365 & Azure** support and **cloud roadmaps**
- 🌐 **Serverless hosting** for websites, blogs, SaaS and internal apps
- 🛡️ **Security**: DNS, email and domain protection, plus vendor solutions we resell and manage

## What's not changing

- **Custom PCs are still available** from our [shop](/shop).
- Our promise: *Simplify technology with tech experts and 99% SLA resolution for your emergency tech support needs.*
