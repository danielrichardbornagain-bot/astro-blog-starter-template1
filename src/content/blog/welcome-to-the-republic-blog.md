**Plug in. Play secure.** That's been the Custom PC Republic motto from day one — and now it has a home for the long version.

## What you'll find here

- 🖥️ **Build logs** — how we spec, assemble and tune every Republic rig
- 🛡️ **Lab notes** — Zero Trust homelab and small-office staging, step by step
- 🌍 **Open source** — progress on [Zamesh](/zamesh), the template collection this blog runs on

## Why a blog?

Most of what we learn happens *at the bench*: a cable route that drops temps by 4°C, a firewall rule that saves a weekend. **Writing it down** means you get the benefit too — whether you buy from the [shop](https://shop.custompcrepublic.com) or build your own.

> Fast to plug in. Safe to play on. That's the whole job.
