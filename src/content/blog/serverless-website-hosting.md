A traditional website lives on **one server** that someone has to patch, back up and watch. When it goes down or gets attacked, the site goes with it.

**Serverless hosting** works differently. Your site is deployed to a **global network** and served from the location nearest each visitor.

## What you get

- ⚡ **Speed**: pages are served from data centres close to your visitors.
- 🛡️ **Security by default**: DDoS protection, TLS and a web application firewall sit in front of everything.
- 🔧 **Nothing to patch**: there's no server operating system for attackers to target.
- 📈 **It scales on its own**: traffic spikes don't need a bigger server.
- 💸 **Predictable cost**: many sites run comfortably on low-cost or free tiers.
