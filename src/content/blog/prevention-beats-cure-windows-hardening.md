Cleaning up an infected computer takes time, costs money and doesn't always fully work. **Hardening** a computer before anything goes wrong is far cheaper.

## Our Windows hardening basics

- 🔄 **Updates on**, for Windows, drivers, browsers and apps.
- 👤 **Standard user accounts** for daily work; admin rights only when needed.
- 🔐 **BitLocker** encryption and a strong sign-in (Windows Hello or a long PIN).
- 🛡️ **Microsoft Defender** tuned properly, or managed protection from **Huntress** or **Malwarebytes** for businesses.
- 🧱 **Firewall and safe defaults**: macros blocked from the internet, unused features turned off.
- 💾 **Backups** that are tested, kept separate from the PC, and protected from ransomware.

## Keep work and play apart

Games, mods, launchers and chat tools add risk to a machine you also use for **work and banking**. Our recommendation:

- 🎮 **Gaming** on its own machine running **Bazzite**, a gaming-focused Linux.
- 💼 **Work** on a clean, hardened **Windows** PC.

If one gets compromised, the other stays safe.
